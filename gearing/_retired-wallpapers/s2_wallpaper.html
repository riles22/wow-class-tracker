Every Week

The list that actually matters
  • Light all nine vault slots — 6 raid bosses, 8 dungeon runs, 8 world or Delve activities.
  • Get one +10 done. It is the last key level that improves anything: 311 from the dungeon, 318 in the vault, and Venomstones start dropping.
  • Cap your crests — 100 of each type. Three of one tier trade up into the next†.
  • Collect your Crafting Spark† and bank it toward a Myth-track craft.
  • Loot the Gilded Stash — four times a week once Delver's Journey hits rank 4. It pays out 5 Myth Mistcrests per run.
  • Take the free bonus roll from Orin Straylight, from week 8 onward.

What Changed

vs. Season 1
  • Heroic raiding matters again. Vault rewards from LFR / Normal / Heroic each climb a full track — Heroic vault is now 318.
  • Season 1 Myth 6/6 (289) lands at Veteran 4/6 in Season 2 terms — replace it fast.
  • Nebulous Voidcore bonus rolls cost 1 coin, down from 2. First one is available Aug 25; a free weekly roll starts week 8.
  • The Catalyst now carries over the original item's secondary stats and cantrip effects onto the tier piece.
  • Pre-season, Aug 11–17: Mythic 0 on a weekly lockout at 292, no keystones, no Bountiful Delves — but vault credit does bank for Aug 18.
  • Max crafted gear reaches 331† — Myth rank 5, not 6.
  • Delver's Journey rank 9 gives Tier 11 Bountiful Coffers a shot at Hero gear. Champion is the default — the Hero roll is an unlock, not a baseline.
  • Ritual Sites tiers 1–6 now pay out vault rewards and crests identical to the matching Delve tier.
