Crafting

Spark of Tides
Per piece
2–4 Sparks
Onto a track
80 Mistcrests
A craft costs 2–4 Sparks of Tides. Adding 80 Mistcrests of a given tier places the finished item on that track — Hero Mistcrests for a Hero-track craft, Myth for Myth.
Max-quality crafted gear tops out at 331† (Myth rank 5, not 6) and qualifies for Ascendant Venomstones, which carry it to 341.

Ritual Sites

Coiled Isle · tiers 1–6
New in Season 2: Ritual Sites tiers 1–6 pay out vault credit and crests identical to the matching Delve tier — a Tier 5 Ritual Site is worth the same as a Tier 5 Delve.
They feed the World row of the Great Vault alongside Delves and Prey, so they are interchangeable filler for the 2 / 4 / 8 thresholds on sheet 1.
