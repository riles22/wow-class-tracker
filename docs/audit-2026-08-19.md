# Audit Report — wow-class-tracker (2026-08-19, post-S2-flip)

## 0. Scope and state

Commissioned the day after the 2026-08-18 Season 2 flip, with three explicit questions:
a comprehensive post-flip audit; *"is any PTR-related stuff interfering UI/UX-wise?"*;
and *"do we still have the S1 locked values somewhere on the screen?"*

State at audit time: HEAD `8f04330`, tree clean. `npm run test:quiet` — 368 tests,
367 pass, 0 fail, 1 skip (the skip is designed self-expiry: the freeze-season "newest
commit still describing the live season" test correctly skips while no frozen records
exist for s2). All 25 UI invariants ran under local Playwright and passed. `npm run
build` exits clean and the local rebuild is **byte-identical to the committed dist/**.
The 2026-08-19 nightly landed green with 12 honestly-degraded manifest rows, each
carrying real evidence. The prior audits' dispositions were treated as closed and not
re-litigated; the owner-accepted standing reds (wcl-live-\*, bloodmallet) are recorded
as accepted, not re-reported.

## 1. Verdict

**The flip itself executed cleanly — every runbook checklist item verified — and the
machinery underneath is in excellent health. The S1 archive lane is exemplary. What the
audit found is one structural inversion and a ring of label rot around it.**

The structural inversion: `showPtr` (`state.era !== "live"`, src/template.html:1575) was
written when "not live" meant *PTR content exists to show*. Post-flip, with `PHASES.ptr`
null, the era is permanently pinned to `"live"` — so everything behind that gate is now
permanently invisible. That is exactly right for genuinely-PTR surfaces (Dummy Dome box,
PTR legend cluster, era toggle — the DECISION 3 sunset, verified fully executed). But
three lanes behind the same gate now carry **live-season content**: the official-tuning
lane (including the Aug-18 launch-day hotfixes the nightly is still logging), the Season 2
tier-set box, and the NEW-badge/fresh-info freshness cues. The tracker collects this data
every night and renders it nowhere. The user-facing symptom is the inverse of the
commissioned question: PTR content is *not* leaking into the live view — live content is
trapped in the retired PTR lane.

## 2. The two direct questions, answered

**S1 locked values: yes — on screen, complete, and provably frozen.** The footer's
"Sources & snapshot dates" column renders "Past seasons: **Season 1 (12.0.7) — final
standings**" as a plain visible link (no JS, no hover dependency) to `s1.html`: all 40
specs in both brackets with rank, consensus letter + 0–100 score, spread marker, and
per-source letters, each column disclosing its lane ("frozen 2026-08-11" vs "snapshot
2026-08-18"). The page is script-free with its own `default-src 'none'` CSP, masthead
chip "SEASON 1 — FINAL RECORD", a gold frozen-record banner, and two ways back to the
live tracker. `dist/s1.html` is **byte-identical to a fresh re-render of
`data/season-archive/s1.json`** (48,933 bytes both sides), the record is Gate-0 immutable
in both directions (modify AND add), append-only is empirically true (one commit ever
touched it), and the ui-invariants href allowlist admits exactly `index|gearing|s1`.
Fifteen checks, zero findings. Nothing to fix here.

**PTR interference: the visible surfaces are largely clean; the defects are the trapped
live content (§3.A) and residual labels (§3.B).** Confirmed clean: era toggle genuinely
hidden (`.seg[hidden]` CSS is real, boot + deep-link sanitizer both pin era to live);
stale `era=ptr`/`fight=` deep links go inert; Fight selector season-gated and hidden
while encounter-tiers is s1; Ladder/Compare/Compare-all drop PTR-era series/columns; all
267 PTR-named metrics carry explicit `era:"ptr"` so none leak into "Current numbers";
icyveins-ptr fully out of registry/ratings/contract; the frozen forecast is named as a
frozen record on every grid surface (viewnote, qualifier, mobile .mtag, drawer, movers,
data-health); gearing.html fully flipped with zero user-visible "PTR" strings.

## 3. Confirmed findings

Ordered by severity. All CONFIRMED items were independently reproduced by an adversarial
verifier; severity shown is the verifier's corrected severity.

### A. The showPtr cluster — live-season content invisible

- **A1 · HIGH · CONFIRMED — the official-tuning lane never renders.** The gold
  "Shipping in 12.1" patch-notes block and every build/hotfix entry — including the live
  2026-08-18 Season-2 launch hotfix round-up — are gated at src/template.html:1607 on
  `showPtr`, permanently false. Meanwhile the masthead promises "click any row for the
  full read: patch changes, …". The nightly logs live hotfixes into `ptr-builds.json`
  (kind `hotfix`) that no drawer will ever show.
- **A2 · MEDIUM (found HIGH) · CONFIRMED — the "Season 2 tier set" box never renders.**
  Sole call site src/template.html:1603, same gate. The live game's actual S2 set
  bonuses — `spec.tierSet`, with a validation gate dedicated to keeping it fresh — are
  invisible to every visitor.
- **A3 · MEDIUM · found, unverified (same mechanism as A1/A2) — NEW badge + fresh-info
  lane permanently dead.** `newBadgeHTML` bails when era is live (src/template.html:1670)
  and the What-changed strip drops its "N specs with fresh info" lane (:2627), while
  their inputs (creator takes, the build/hotfix feed) are now live-season content
  arriving daily — the 08-19 Resto Druid take and the 08-18 hotfixes trigger nothing.
- **A4 · refuted → INFO — the writeup lane placeholder is NOT a defect.** All 39 `ptr`
  writeups render as "12.1 baseline view — Live-season data." post-flip; the verifier
  confirmed the behavior but established it as the decided, documented flip consequence.
  Recorded here only because A1–A3 fixes should not accidentally resurrect it.

The common shape of any fix: these lanes need to be gated on **what the content is**
(kind/era of the entry) rather than on **which era view is selected** — the era toggle
is a *view* switch and its retirement should not have taken live content with it.

## 5. Why the tests didn't catch A1–A3

Not a regression: UI invariants 324/325 pin that the frozen forecast renders and that
PTR-derived surfaces hide in the live view — hiding is exactly what the gate does. The
defect is a *classification* gap (live-season content living in a lane keyed to the
retired era **view**), which no invariant models. Any A-cluster fix should add one: "a
kind:hotfix entry dated after launch is reachable in some drawer".
