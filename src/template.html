How to read itWhere every spec of WoW: Midnight stands — not one site's opinion, but every major tier list distilled into a single consensus with a ± wherever they disagree, layered with live numbers, sim profiles, and per-spec patch reads. Several tier lists feed a computed consensus, with a ± marker where they disagree — flip between them with View, or pick Ours: 12.1 ✦ for this site's own Season-2 forecast (a projection, never a source). Filter, sort, or search the roster, and click any row for the full read: patch changes, current numbers with ranks, sim fight profile, and community takes. Two tools up top: 📊 The Ladder draws any measured ranking as a chart, and ⊞ Compare all is the whole roster against every rating and rank on one screen. The ⚙ S2 Gearing tab is a separate explorer for Season-2 gear and loot sources.
